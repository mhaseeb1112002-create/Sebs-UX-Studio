Mistakes I see

8 mistakes that quietly lose users

Most agencies list services. I’d rather show you what goes wrong, what people see, and the fix.

Seen in every industry I work in

  1. Asking for a document with no example

    FixShow an example and the matching rules before the upload starts.

    What people see

    People don’t know what a good upload looks like, so they retry. Format or name mismatches only show up after a rejection.

  2. Rejecting with “something went wrong”

    FixSay what failed, give one next step, and keep their progress.

    What people see

    A message like “an issue with your identity verification” and nothing else.

  3. Leaving no way out

    FixOffer another route: a different document, a human, or come back later.

    What people see

    Retry loops that end at a support form.

  4. Mistaking setup for value

    FixPick one first win and show it before the setup.

    What people see

    Profile photos, team invites and an empty dashboard before anything useful happens.

  5. One onboarding for everyone, then silence

    FixAsk one question to route people, and nudge them when they stall.

    What people see

    Every new user gets the same tour, and nobody follows up after login.

  6. Asking for too much before showing value

    FixAsk only what the next step needs. Let people look around first.

    What people see

    Long forms and forced accounts before anyone has seen what the product does.

  7. A website that tells a different story from sales

    FixKeep one story from the first page to the demo call, and offer a smaller first step than a demo.

    What people see

    69% of B2B buyers report that the website and the sellers disagree (Gartner, 2025).

  8. Blaming the page when the problem is who arrives

    FixCheck who is arriving and why before changing screens. A good audit tells you when the answer isn’t design.

    What people see

    Teams redesign a page that is fine while the wrong visitors keep arriving.
