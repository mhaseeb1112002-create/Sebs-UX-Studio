Fintech onboarding, step by step

A concept project that shows exactly how I work, from the audit to the redesigned flow. The product is a mockup. The process is the one I use with every client.

  1. 1

    Audit the flow

    I go through signup and ID verification screen by screen, and mark every mistake with a pin.

    You get: Annotated screens

  2. 2

    Find the biggest leak

    Each issue is ranked by impact, so you fix the step that loses the most users first.

    You get: Drop-off map

  3. 3

    Redesign the step

    Example photo first, errors in plain words, and a way out when a check fails. Compliance stays the same.

    You get: Before and after prototype

  4. 4

    Hand off and measure

    Specs and components for your developers, plus the numbers we agreed to track.

    You get: Dev-ready specs
