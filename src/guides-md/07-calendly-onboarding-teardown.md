I reviewed Calendly's sign-up screen by screen, from the first click to a link you can share. It takes 9 steps to reach the dashboard.

| Step | Screen |
|---|---|
| 1 | Email |
| 2 | Name + password |
| 3 | Check inbox |
| 4 | Log in again |
| 5 | Scheduling URL |
| 6 | Connect calendar |
| 7 | Available hours |
| 8 | Role |
| 9 | Dashboard (first value) |

Most of it is excellent. Two steps are not.

## What works

**One field to start.** Email is the only field. Type a Gmail address and Calendly offers Google sign-up, which also connects your calendar.

**Smart defaults you can skip.** Available hours are pre-filled (9 to 5, Monday to Friday), and most setup screens offer "Set up later".

**Something ready on arrival.** A 30-minute meeting type already exists on the dashboard, with Copy link and Share. You have something useful in seconds. (One small miss: the checklist still asks you to "Create an event type".)

## The wall: steps 3 and 4

Right after you set a password, you leave the product to confirm your email in your inbox, then come back and log in again.

That's two steps outside the product before any value. Each one is a place to give up: the email lands late, goes to spam, or the person gets distracted in their inbox.

## The fix

Send the confirmation link, but let people keep going. Show a quiet notice like "We sent a link to jane@company.com. Confirm it anytime today." and confirm the email in the background.

People reach their first meeting link first. The check still happens, just not as a wall.

## What to take from it

1. One field to start, and offer Google sign-up.
2. Pre-fill setup, and let people skip it.
3. Give them something ready to use.
4. Verify email in the background, not as a wall.

*Flow reviewed in November 2026 from a recorded sign-up; Calendly may have changed it since. The fix is a concept, not Calendly's design.*
