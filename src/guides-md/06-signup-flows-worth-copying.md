A new user opens your sign-up. Before they see one useful screen, you ask for their company size, phone number and role.

Most of them never reach your product. Not because it's bad, but because you asked too early.

The first check in every sign-up audit I run:

1. List every field and step before the first useful screen.
2. Next to each one, write when you actually need it.
3. Move everything marked "later" to after the first win.

The products below already work this way. Each gets one thing right, and each gives you one fix.

## 1. Duolingo: value before the account
You finish a first lesson before it asks you to make an account.
**Fix:** show value first. Ask for the account after the first win.

## 2. Calendly: one field to start
Just an email, or one tap with Google, which also connects your calendar.
**Fix:** cut sign-up to one field. Ask the rest later, when it's needed.

## 3. Stripe: use it before you verify
You can test everything in a sandbox. Business checks come only when you go live.
**Fix:** delay heavy checks until the moment they're actually needed.

## 4. Wise: says why it asks
"We may need to ask for proof of this address." No surprise later.
**Fix:** under every sensitive field, add one line on why you need it.

## 5. Monzo: explains ID checks plainly
The video selfie is to check it's really you, and used for nothing else.
**Fix:** say what you'll do with personal data, and what you won't.

## 6. Slack: no password to forget
You can sign in with a code sent to your email.
**Fix:** offer passwordless or Google sign-in. Fewer resets, fewer drop-offs.

## 7. Notion: one question, then it adapts
It asks what you'll use it for and sets up the workspace to match.
**Fix:** ask one question at sign-up, and use the answer on the first screen.

## 8. Airtable: no blank first screen
Describe what you need and it builds a starting base with your fields.
**Fix:** never land new users on an empty page. Give them a starting point.

## 9. Figma: the example is already there
"Figma Basics" files are waiting in your workspace on first login.
**Fix:** teach with a real example file, not a product tour.

## 10. Loom: value in minutes
Built so you start recording in minutes and share in a few clicks.
**Fix:** time your sign-up to first value. Cut every step that isn't needed.

## Which one first?

Start with the fix that matches your biggest drop. If people leave before the first screen, start with Duolingo and Calendly. If they leave at ID checks, start with Wise and Monzo. If they sign up and never come back, start with Notion, Airtable and Figma.
