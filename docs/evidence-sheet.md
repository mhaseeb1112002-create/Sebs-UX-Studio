# Evidence sheet

Source of truth for the claims used in positioning and copy. Same data as `docs/evidence-sheet.csv` and the private page https://claude.ai/artifact/Tap8eDsjpNnuMF1gt2ptuV.

How to read: I saw search summaries and, for a few items, the publisher's own headline. "Primary" means the headline figure matched the publisher's release. For anything else, check the original before it goes on the site or in a pitch.

Use: **Quote with source** = safe when you name the source, year and sample. **Paraphrase, no number** = use the idea in plain words. **Context only** = background. **Do not quote** = unverified or conflicting.
